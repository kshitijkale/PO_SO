candidates up to the parent's level but can't exceed when the parent already
solves the "solvable" problems. Ceiling effect of tiny minibatches.

6. ISSUES FOUND
---------------

6a. Refinement steps not logged to log.jsonl
  The self.logger.log() messages go to stdout, not JSONL. The on_refinement_step
  callback events also don't appear — ResearchLogger doesn't handle them yet.
  Impact: Can't observe per-step score progression from logs.

6b. run_log.md shows inconsistent scores
  Shows "New prompt score: 0/3 (old: 3/3)" for iter 1, but actual decision
  event shows old=2, new=3. ResearchLogger rendering bug.

6c. Minibatch size limits refinement ceiling
  With 3 examples and binary scores, score space is {0,1,2,3}. When parent
  solves 2-3 problems, no room for refinement to improve. Consider minibatch=5-6
  when using refinement.


7. RECOMMENDATIONS
